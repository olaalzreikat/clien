ABOUT ME!

HANA TAYLOR

Hello! I am an illustrator, animator and filmmaker based in Arizona. I am currently studying for a Bachelors Degree in Animation. Since high school I started making and working on animated short films, now they are my favorite form of storytelling.

I also illustrate fun, colorful and whimsical illustrations! Find yourself getting lost in the endless details and hidden characters in my art.

  • PUSD Film Festival 2024 and 2025 2× Winner
  • Rocky Mountain Southwest Chapter Student Production Award 2024 and 2025 2× Winner
  • National Academy of Television Arts & Sciences' National Student Production Award Winner
  • Huntington University Arizona Highschool Illustration Competition 2025 3rd Place
