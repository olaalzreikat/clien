Payment Method

All payments through PayPal or Cashapp (USD only). Include your name/username and commission request with payment.

Payment Terms

50% upfront before I start. Remaining 50% once you approve the low resolution final. Let me know of any major changes before final payment.

Completion Time

First come, first serve. I am not a full time artist. Orders may take 1 week to several months. I will keep you updated at every stage. Have a deadline? Let me know!

Refunds

Refunds available before I start. Emergency refunds based on progress stage:

Sketch90% back
Lineart70% back
Flat Color50% back
Shading / Details10% back

Full refund if I cannot complete the commission.

Progress Updates

Updates at every stage. Major & minor changes free during Sketch. Minor changes free during later stages. Major changes after Sketch = +5% to total price.
